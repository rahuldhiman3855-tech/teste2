9 Future Directions: The Path to Hybrid Intelligence

Agentic AI systems are rapidly evolving beyond static task automation into dynamic, collabo- rative, and adaptive entities [157]. Their future development will hinge on interdisciplinary ad- vances, technological convergence, and—critically—a paradigm-aware approach to design that seeks to integrate the strengths of both symbolic and neural lineages into robust hybrid architec- tures.
