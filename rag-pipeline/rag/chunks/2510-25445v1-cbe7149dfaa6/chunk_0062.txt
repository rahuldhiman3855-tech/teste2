Conclusion The future of Agentic AI is a synthesis. Its trajectory will be shaped not only by tech- nical breakthroughs but by thoughtful, paradigm-aware integration of ethics, interdisciplinary methods, and infrastructure-aware governance [9]. The next conceptual turning point will be defined by our ability to engineer hybrid intelligence—systems that are both adaptable and re- liable, both creative and sound. The question is no longer whether agents will become intelligent partners, but whether we can architect a future of hybrid intelligence that is both powerful and trustworthy.
