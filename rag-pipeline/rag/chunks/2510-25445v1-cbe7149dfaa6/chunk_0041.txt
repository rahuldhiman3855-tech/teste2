5.1 Tool Use and Capabilities: Integration with Real-World Sys- tems

A critical capability that distinguishes agentic AI from passive models is their ability to inter- act with and manipulate external tools and data sources via Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) [109, 110, 111, 112]. This functionality is the bridge between an agent’s internal rea- soning and tangible action in the real world. The nature of this integration is, as our framework predicts, paradigm-dependent.
