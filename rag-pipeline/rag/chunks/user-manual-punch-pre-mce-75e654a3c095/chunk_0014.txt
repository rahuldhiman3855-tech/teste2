Media

The infotainment system supports the following media sources from which you can play music, videos, and view pictures:

• USB

• Bluetooth Audio

• iPhone as an iPod

• Android Auto

Note: The supported audio formats aac, .mp3, .wav and .wma.

• Apple CarPlay

Note: The supported video formats are .mp4 and .avi.
