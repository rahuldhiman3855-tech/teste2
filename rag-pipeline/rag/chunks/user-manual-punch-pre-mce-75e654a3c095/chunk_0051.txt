Audio Settings

The system allows you to manage your in-vehicle audio experience. With Audio Settings you can:

• Setup Volume for Media, Phone, Ringtone and System

• Control Bass, Middle and Treble using Audio Equalizer

• Manage in-vehicle volume distribution among the speakers using Balance and Fader settings
