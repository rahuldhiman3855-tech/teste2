If the vehicle is equipped with this feature, you can ON/OFF DRL (Daytime Running Lamps) from the infotainment system.

To set the Approach lights illumination time:

To ON/OFF DRL:

1. In the Vehicle Settings screen, navigate to and select the Approach Lamps option. The system displays the various illumination time options.

1. In the Vehicle Setup screen, navigate to and select the Lights option.

2. In the Lighting screen tap on DRL tap the DRL bar icon to ON/OFF DRLs.

2. Navigate to and select the required illumination time option from 30 seconds/60 seconds/90 seconds.

TATA Assist Call (Breakdown Call)*
[IMAGE: rag/assets/user-manual-punch-pre-mce-75e654a3c095/page_072_img_001.jpeg]

In case of vehicle break down, the infotainment system helps you to reach you to TATA Motors service centre.

Note: This feature requires active inbuilt internet connectivity in the car.

1. Tap the Break Call application, from the App launcher Screen.

2. Tap B Call button, the infotainment system dials the TATA Break Down Service centre.

3. Swipe right on the ‘Slide to End Call’ bar to disconnect the call.
