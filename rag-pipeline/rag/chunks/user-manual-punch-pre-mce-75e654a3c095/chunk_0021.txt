Connecting your phone with the infotainment system is called as pairing. The infotainment system supports a maximum of 10 paired devices. A device once paired need not be paired again unless the pairing is deleted. A phone with an existing pairing with the infotainment system will be automatically connected when Bluetooth is enabled in both the devices and the phone is within the range of the system.
