VEHICLE FUNCTIONS Lighting

Figure 51 : Approach lamp

Lighting
[IMAGE: rag/assets/user-manual-punch-pre-mce-75e654a3c095/page_071_img_001.jpeg]

The infotainment system allows you to control lighting features like Approach Lamp and DRL if your vehicle is equipped with these features.

Approach Lamp

When you approach your car in the dark, you can switch on the Approach Lamps (some exterior and interior lights of the vehicle) to identify your car as well as to find your way safely to it.

DRL (Daytime Running Lamps)*

The system allows you to set the time for which the Approach lamps will be illuminated after you switch them ON.
