Android Auto is a projection technology, which allows you to operate your Android smartphones from your vehicle infotainment system. You can use voice actions or simple arrangement of Google Maps, and other important phone functions to access the required functionalities. It is designed to minimize distraction while driving.

Note: Android Auto is by default enabled on your vehicle.

Connect - Wireless Android Auto

1. Pair/connect your phone to the infotainment system using Bluetooth®.

The Android Auto feature requires the installation of the Android Auto Mobile App, which can be downloaded on your smartphone from Google Play Store.

2. Follow the prompts on the screen and on your phone to start.

3. You will be asked to accept Terms before using the feature.

Note: Applicable only for Android Smartphones with Android Version 10 and above
